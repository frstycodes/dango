Sign in and states

Signing in, and the app-wide empty, loading, error and offline states. Every wait shows the mascot or the mini wave and names what it waits on; every failure says what happened and offers one way forward.

220×248, radius 14 (--radius-md), no window controls, not movable. The card is the page neutral (graphite) with a hairline; dark and light follow the system theme. The mark (the logo's own bar proportions, static) and "Wave" (22, heavy) are carved: filled a step darker than the card, shaded along their upper inner edge and caught by light below. Stage 12 medium, muted, one line, truncated. The progress bar (120×4, accent on a faint track) moves a third per stage, and shows real download progress while an update installs. Stages advance 320ms apart once servers arrive; the card never outstays 25s. A drop during launch sets the line back to "Reconnecting…".

Sign-in window

Signing in happens on your phone: Wave shows a code, Discord's app scans it, and you approve there. Each phase's card springs up into place so every step feels like it arrived. The whole window drags.

Title 20 bold, one line; detail muted, 34ch. Mascot moods: preparing and captcha wait (ripple), ready and failed breathe, scanned and signed in show the person instead. The QR card is white in both themes or phones can't read it; modules are warm ink squircles with the wave mark in the cleared centre (high error correction covers it). Cards arrive from 12px below at 0.97 over 260ms on --ease-spring; reduced motion shows them in place.

Token and two-factor

The other way in sits behind a disclosure under the code, with the risk spelled out before the field. Two-factor asks for six digits in one paste.

Disclosure: one chevron, right when closed and turned down when open; the content folds as one block (height and fade, 200ms, --ease-out). Warning callout before the field, never after. GAP: src/login.rs has no token or two-factor step today; these are the screens for when Discord answers a sign-in with an MFA ticket and for a pasted token.

Captcha hand-off
