Better than Discord

Where Wave's panels part ways with Discord's, and why.

Discord doesWave doesWhy

Pins and the threads browser open as popovers over the chat and close on the first click outside.

Pins and threads dock in the side panelSame frame as members and search, resizable, open beside the chat.

You can read a pin or skim threads while following the conversation, and one frame means one way to open, resize and close.

Unpinning asks for confirmation in a dialog.

Unpin is instant, with Undo in placeThe row becomes "Unpinned Jun's message · Undo".

Unpinning is reversible, so a dialog is a needless step; the undo sits where your eyes already are.

Marking a channel read in Unreads removes it at once, with no way back.

Mark read leaves a one-line record with UndoMark all read does the same for the tab.

A misclick on a long unread list loses your place; the record keeps it recoverable without a confirm.

Mentions filters (@everyone, roles, this server) sit in a menu behind an icon.

Filters are visible chipsToggled in one click, state always on show.

A hidden filter leaves you wondering why a mention is missing; a chip shows what's on.

Active now disappears when the window is narrow.

Active now folds into a strip above the listSwipeable name cards under 820px.

Who's playing is the reason many people open Home; it should survive a smaller window.

No results shows an illustration and a "nothing found" line.

No results suggests what to dropOne-click chips remove a filter or widen to all channels, plus a matching tip.

An empty result is a dead end unless it says how to get a result.

A thread deleted while you have it open closes, and a reply you were typing goes with it.

The panel stays and says so"This thread was deleted", with your unsent reply kept to copy or move to the channel.

Nothing you typed is lost, and nothing changes without a reason you can read.

The member list has a fixed width.

The side panel resizesDrag or arrow keys, 200 to 380px, double-click to reset.

Long names and wide search results need room on big screens; small screens need the chat back.

The Discord column describes its desktop client from use, not from a fresh check; confirm a row against current Discord before quoting it.
