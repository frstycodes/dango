Channel settings window

Edit channel opens this over the whole window, like server settings: the channel heads the nav, then Overview, Permissions, Invites and Webhooks, then Delete channel. Pages you can't use are left out, not greyed: Permissions needs Manage permissions on the channel, Invites needs Manage channel, Webhooks needs Manage webhooks. Edits on Overview and Permissions collect in one save bar; Discord's own refusal shows in it word for word.

Voice and stage overview

Voice channels trade topic for sound: bitrate up to the server's boost cap, a member limit, camera quality and a region. Region lists Discord's live regions after Automatic.

Permissions

The basic switch first: Private channel, which denies View channel to @everyone. Under it, server settings' channel overrides as they are: a notice says whether the channel matches its category, the targets list on the left, each permission as deny, inherit or allow. Only permissions that apply to this channel type are listed.

Invites to this channel only, with uses and expiry, each revocable. Webhooks list by name with Copy URL and Delete. Both load when opened; a failure says Discord's words and offers Try again.
