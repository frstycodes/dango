DM call player

Calls in DMs and group DMs as one floating window over whatever you're reading, the way Teams' minimized meeting window, Slack's huddle mini window and Meet's picture-in-picture work. It rings in the corner, becomes the call in place, grows when someone turns on video, and opens into the server voice stage when you want the big view. Drag it to any corner; everything in it works.

How other apps do it

  1. Microsoft Teams: minimizing a meeting leaves a small meeting window in the corner, with an expanded size (up to four videos) and a compact one (no video). Per Microsoft's support page and message center notice MC1266027.
  2. Slack huddles: a huddle runs in a bar at the foot of the sidebar and pops out into a mini window. The pop-out is from a ZDNet guide; the current button names weren't checked.
  3. Google Meet, FaceTime on Mac: the call continues as picture-in-picture or its own small window while you work elsewhere. From memory, not checked this session.
  4. Taken from them: the call is one object that floats above the conversation instead of taking it over, has two sizes (with and without video), and goes big only when you ask.

What it means in the Rust code

  1. Controls. The stage keeps the full grouped bar the main session is building in call.rs. The player renders the same control buttons at a compact size (32px), only mute, camera and share in one group, plus leave.
  2. The player is pip.rs grown up. It already floats over the app, rests in a corner and springs to the nearest one when dropped. It becomes the DM call's window: a head (name, timer, open stage, more), then faces or video, then four compact controls: mute, camera, share, leave. Device menus, activities and the rest stay on the stage. Portrait by default (248px wide; faces and cameras crop to 3:4); it widens to 16:9 only for a shared screen, switching on its own when a stream starts or stops.
  3. The strip goes. dm_call.rs::dm_call_strip and its reserved height in chat.rs are removed; the feed gets its full height back. incoming_call_banner becomes the player's ringing state, in the same corner, so answering doesn't move anything.
  4. Joinable calls. StripState::Joinable becomes a pill in the DM header (channel_header): faces, a camera mark if anyone's on video, and Join. Nothing floats until you join.
  5. The stage is the server's. Open stage shows render_call for the DM channel exactly as for a server voice channel; nothing DM-specific is drawn there. Its header gets one new control, Back to chat, which returns to the player (Esc too). Full screen stays set_call_fullscreen.
  6. Ending. The player reads "Call ended · 12 minutes" for ~1.5s, then shrinks away. The call record line in chat.rs (already there) carries Call again or Call back.
