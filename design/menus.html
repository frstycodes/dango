Keyboard

Shift+F10 or the menu key opens the menu for what has focus, with the first row focused. Arrows move, wrapping at the ends; disabled rows are skipped. Every menu on this page takes these keys: click into one and try.

Better than Discord

Where these menus differ from Discord's on purpose. Discord's side is read from Wave's Rust port of its menus (src/*_menu.rs), which mirrors Discord's menu code.

Discord doesWave doesWhy

Mute lengths say how long, not until when. Once muted, the row reads "Muted until 11/15/2026, 9:41 PM".

Cm muteEach length shows its end ("Until 11:41 PM"); muted reads "Muted until 9:41 PM" or "tomorrow at…".

You pick a mute by when you'll be back, and today's date is noise.

Rows you can't use are greyed with no reason (Mark as read, Mute thread before joining, roles above yours).

Cm threadEvery disabled row has a second line saying why: "Join the thread to mute it", "Above your highest role", "It's here now".

A grey row with no reason looks broken; the reason tells you what to do.

Moving a channel is two levels deep: Move to… › Category › list. Server moves offer only first or last in the list, or a folder.

Folders and movingOne submenu: Move to category, or Move server with top, bottom, after any server, or into a folder. The current spot is marked.

Fewer hover hops, and you can put a server anywhere without dragging.

Deleting your own message asks "Are you sure you want to delete this message?"

Cm ownYour own message goes at once with a 6s Undo toast. Someone else's still asks, with the message shown.

A dialog for a thing you do daily trains you to click through it; Undo is safer and faster.

Confirms ask "Are you sure…?" and the button is a bare verb: "Leave Server", "Remove All", "Kick".

ConfirmsThe body says what happens; buttons name both outcomes with the target: "Stay" / "Leave Night Shift", "Remove 14 reactions".

You can decide from the button alone, and the count shows the size of the mistake.

Timeout is a submenu of six lengths: no end time, no reason.

ConfirmsA dialog with each length's end time, an optional reason, and a button that says "Time out for 1 hour".

Moderators need the reason in the audit log and should see exactly when it lifts.
