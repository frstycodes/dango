Upload preview

Opens on drop or paste, before anything sends. Every file is in one list: rename it, mark it as a spoiler, or remove it, then add a comment and upload. Hold Shift while dropping to skip it.

64px thumbnails at 11 radius; the name field keeps the extension as a fixed suffix so a rename can't break the file. Spoiler blurs the thumbnail here as it will in chat. A file over the limit stays listed with why, and is left out of the count on the Upload button. Removing the last file closes the dialog.
