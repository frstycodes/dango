color

Discord's 4px bar down the left edge, in the exact colour the bot sent. No colour: the strong hairline tone.

provider

12 faint, above everything. Link previews set it; bots rarely do.

author

24px round icon, 14 semibold, one line with an ellipsis. A url makes the name a link.

title + url

16 bold. With a url it takes the accent ink and links; without, it stays strong text.

description

14 / 1.45 with Discord markdown: headers, subtext, quotes, lists, code, masked links, mentions, timestamps, spoilers. Past ~12 lines it clips under a fade with Show more.

fields

Name 12 semibold muted over value 14 strong. Consecutive inline fields share a row: three across, two beside a thumbnail. A field without inline takes the full row.

thumbnail

80 × 80 at the top right, beside the text, never above the image.

image / video

The full width of the card at its own shape, up to 360 tall. Video adds the play button; embeds sharing a url fold into one gallery of up to four.

footer + timestamp

20px icon, 12 faint, then the time as Today, Yesterday or a date, joined by a middle dot.
