Principles

The wave is the voice

Speaking, typing, loading and live all draw the bars, never a generic spinner or ring.

Magenta means you

Accent goes to your actions, your selection and what asks for you: mentions, live, unread counts.

Ink and bone

Warm neutrals: plum-tinted ink at night, paper bone by day. No cool blue-greys.

Soft, not round

Squircles for servers, buttons and tiles; pills only for counts, status and the bars.

Colour

Restrained: warm neutrals plus one magenta. Depth in the dark theme comes from lighter surfaces, never shadows. Contrast figures are measured live against the page.

One family for the interface, a mono for code. Switch the typeface at the top to compare; Gabarito is the proposal: geometric and friendly like the bars, and nobody else's.

Space and shape

A 4px scale. Controls 10, surfaces 14, dialogs 20; nested corners stay concentric.

Motion

Quick and a little springy. Things you touch overshoot slightly and settle inside 250ms; nothing that happens a hundred times a day animates more than a fade. Click a card to play it.

Icons

Phosphor, regular for rows and chrome, bold for buttons, fill for state (on, live, playing). Rounded joins match the bars.

Controls

Verb plus object on every button. Hover, press, focus (Tab to it), disabled and loading are all live.

Status is never colour alone: online is a dot, idle a moon, do not disturb a bar, offline a ring. Speaking is the accent ring plus the bars.
