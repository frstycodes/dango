DM call flow

A proposal for 1:1 and group DM calls, stage by stage in a real-size window. Behaviour and sounds stay Discord's: a call rings for about 30 seconds, it keeps running while you browse elsewhere, and adding people makes a group. The screens are ours.

IdeaWhat it meansWhy

The chat stays the screen

A voice call is a 56px strip under the DM header: faces, timer, controls. The stage only opens when someone turns on a camera or shares a screen, and it resizes.

Most DM calls are two people talking while they send links. Discord gives half the DM to two avatars on black.

Ringing never blocks

An incoming call drops a banner from the top of what you're looking at. It never takes focus, so typing can't answer or end it. The DM row and the rail say "Calling you" too.

You decide when to look; nothing jumps in front of the message you're writing.

The call goes where you go

Leave the DM and the call lives in the sidebar's voice panel, the rail and the DM row. With video on, a small player rides along in the corner.

Three places say where your call is, and each is one click from it.

Every ending leaves a line

Declined, no answer, missed and finished calls each leave one system line in the DM, with Call back or Call again on it.

The history answers "did they call?" without a notification hunt.

Step by step

Pick a step, then a case. Everything in the window works: rows hover, mute toggles, the add-people checks tick. ← and → move between steps while the step list has focus.
