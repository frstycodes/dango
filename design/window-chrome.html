Window frame

One frame and one rule. The first 48px of every column is its header, and the headers line up across the window. Columns are told apart by tone: the navigation and the members sit on the sidebar tone, the content on the page tone. There are no hairlines, cards or extra strips. The content header floats over the feed and blurs it as the feed scrolls under. The only thing that changes between platforms is what fills the rail's slot in the header row: the traffic lights, or the Home tile.
