Window chrome

No separate title bar. The rail and channel list are one surface. On macOS a slim strip over them holds the traffic lights, then the open server's name in 14px, which drops the server menu. The strip exists only for the lights: on Windows, and on macOS in full screen, the rail and channel list run to the window's top, and the server name heads the channel list at 16px. The content column runs up to the window's top, and its header doubles as the title bar, blurring the feed as it scrolls under. Every header and the strip drag the window. On Windows the caption buttons sit flush at the top right, over the content header, and the header's tools keep clear of them. History has no buttons: ⌘[ / ⌘] on macOS, Alt+←/→ on Windows, and the mouse's side buttons.
