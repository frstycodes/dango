Block or ignore?
Block
  • They can't DM you or send friend requests
  • Their messages in servers are hidden behind a line
  • They see you as offline and can tell they're blocked
Ignore
  • Their messages collapse; their DMs go quiet
  • No notifications from them
  • They aren't told and can still message you
