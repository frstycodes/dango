Better than Discord

Where Discord's messaging flows are weak, Wave does this instead. Discord's side is described from its current desktop app.

Discord doesWave doesWhy

If the message you're replying to is deleted, the reply bar gives no clear account of what happens to your reply.

Reply bar that reportsThe bar says the original was deleted, keeps your text and sends it as a plain message.

You learn it before sending, and nothing you typed is lost.

Replying to someone you blocked looks like any other reply.

Blocked replyThe ping toggle locks off and a line says they'll still see the reply.

Blocking is about contact; the composer should respect it and say so.

Editing a message down to nothing opens a modal asking whether to delete it.

Empty editThe editor's own line turns into Delete message or Keep editing.

Inline where the decision is; no modal for a two-way choice.

If a moderator deletes a message while you edit it, the edit goes with it.

Deleted while editingYour edited text moves into the composer.

Never throw away what someone typed.

Deleting your own message asks for confirmation unless you hold Shift, and there is no undo.

Delete with UndoInstant, with a 6-second Undo toast; only someone else's message asks first, with a preview.
