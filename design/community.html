Channels

Hover: Skip the rest replaces the dots

Closing the questions midway keeps what you answered and leaves this row first in the list. It opens the dialog on the first unanswered question.

The dots are the dialog's own progress: filled for answered, ringed for left. The full count is the row's description and tooltip.

Skip the rest takes the server's defaults for what's left; the row becomes "Using defaults · Undo" for 6 seconds. Answers can always be changed later from the server menu.

Better than Discord

Where this page departs from Discord on purpose. Discord's side is described from how its client behaves today and may have drifted.

Discord doesWave doesWhy

Lists templates by name only.

CreateEach template names the channels it makes.

You pick knowing the result, instead of deleting channels after.

The new-server checklist includes downloading the app.

ChecklistThree steps that matter, with a 1 of 3 count; done steps stay visible as done.

You're already in the app; progress shows at a glance.

Joins first, then shows the server; failures share one "invalid or expired" line.

JoinPreviews the server as you paste and names each failure: not found, expired, banned, full, too many servers, already in.

Errors say what happened and the next step, often as the one button.

Link settings replace the invite dialog with a second one.

InviteSettings fold open inside the same dialog, with a one-line summary when closed.

No modal on a modal; you see the link change as you edit.

The invite targets the channel you opened it from.

Picker"They land in" is a picker right in the title line.

Changing the destination doesn't mean closing and reopening elsewhere.

Closing onboarding halfway leaves no way back to the questions short of the server menu.

Finish joining rowKept answers, a row first in the channel list with the same progress dots, and Skip the rest with Undo.

Unfinished work stays visible where you'll look, and can be finished or dropped in one step.

Paused invites show up as a failure on the joiner's end.

Paused, said in the dialogThe invite dialog names the pause and when it ends; a moderator resumes it right there.

The person inviting learns why before sending a dead link.

Onboarding answers don't say what they give you.

QuestionsEach pick shows the channels and role it adds before you move on.

You choose with the consequence in view.

Rules gating greys the composer and points to a separate banner.

RulesThe composer itself holds the ask; pressing Accept without ticking says what's missing.

The ask sits where you tried to act; no disabled button with no reason.

Channel browsing toggles one channel at a time.

ChannelsPer-category select-all, a live "You see 6 of 10" count, and locked channels say why.

An all-at-once path beside the per-item one.

A raised hand is only an icon state; requests live in a separate tab.

StageThe listener bar says your state in words; moderators get requests, a stop switch and Invited state in one popover.

Status never by icon alone; the moderator's queue is where they act.

Owners don't get Leave; transfer lives deep in member settings.

OwnershipLeave opens a transfer picker, or a delete when you're alone.

The blocker resolves in the dialog that names it.

Leaving is final.

UndoAn Undo toast where rejoining needs no invite.

Undo beats a confirm for recoverable actions.

A kicked server silently vanishes; an unavailable one just greys.

StatesKicked: it fades, the rail closes up, a toast says why. Unavailable: a mark, a tooltip and a pane that say it'll come back.

Things don't change without a reason you can read.
