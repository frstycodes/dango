Settings window

Settings take the whole window: who you are and a search at the top of the nav, one page on the right with a big title. Voice and video lists its sections under it. Close with the button or Esc; changes save as you make them.

Live: every nav item opens its page (built from the page sections below), the four links under Voice and video jump within it, search filters rows across all pages, and Log out asks in place. Nav 240px on the sidebar tone, rows 32px at 14px with an 18px icon, group labels 12px semibold. Content max 680px, 32px between groups. The close button stays pinned top right as the page scrolls.

Narrow window

Under 680px of its own width the window shows one column. The nav becomes a full-width list with chevrons; a page pushes in from the right over it and a back button returns.

Push travels 260ms on --ease-drawer: the page slides in from 100%, the list drifts to −30% under it; with reduced motion the swap is instant. Rows grow to 44px for touch. The bar holds back and close; the page keeps its big title.

Settings search

Search matches every row by its name, description and a few synonyms, on every page, as you type. Results are the real rows, grouped under their page, so a switch can be flipped right there. The nav keeps only pages with matches and counts them.

Better than Discord

Wave keeps every setting Discord has and changes how a few of them work, where Discord hides an action, leaves a state unexplained or spends a dialog on one decision.

Settings search
Discord does
Filters the page names in the sidebar.
Wave does
Matches every setting by name, description and synonyms, and shows the rows themselves, live, grouped by page.
Why
You change a setting without knowing which page it lives on.
Noise suppression
Discord does
A dropdown hides the three choices behind a click.
Wave does
A segmented control shows None, Standard and Advanced at once.
Why
Three options fit in a row: one click instead of two, and the current one is always visible.
Disabled settings
Discord does
Greys out Advanced voice activity with no reason.
Wave does
The row says what it needs: automatic sensitivity in voice activity mode.
Why
A dead control that doesn't say why reads as broken.
No audio detected
Discord does
Asks you to check you've selected the right input device.
Wave does
Names the device, lists the likely causes, and offers Choose another input and Open System Settings.
Why
An error says how to fix it, with the fix one click away.
Sound previews
Discord does
A "Preview sound" text link under each cue's name.
Wave does
A play button beside each switch that turns into the wave while it plays.
Why
One column to scan, and you can see which sound is playing.
Removing a keybind
Discord does
A small X appears only on hover at the card's corner and removes at once.
Wave does
A remove button on every row, always visible, with Undo in the row's place.
Why
No action is hidden behind hover, and a slip costs one click.
Shortcut conflicts
Discord does
Lets two keybinds share one shortcut without a word.
Wave does
Names the keybind that has it and offers Move it here; another app's shortcut gets an error with the fix.
Why
One key doing two things is a bug you find mid-call.
Key names
Discord does
Spells keys in capitals: CMD + K.
Wave does
Keycaps with the platform's symbols: ⌘ K.
Why
Matches the menus and the keyboard, and reads at a glance.
Reset and log out
Discord does
Opens a modal to ask if you're sure.
Wave does
The row asks in place, naming what happens; reset then offers Undo.
Why
A single reversible decision doesn't need a dialog over the page.
Display name
Discord does
Edit opens a modal with one field.
Wave does
The row becomes the field; Enter saves, Esc cancels, the new name shows at once.
Why
One field, edited where you read it.
User ID
Discord does
Hides it until you turn on Developer Mode, then a right-click copies it.
Wave does
Shows it in Account info with Copy ID and a Copied state.
Why
Support, bots and friends ask for it; no hidden mode to find first.
Theme picker
Discord does
Plain colour discs.
Wave does
A small window in each theme, and the neutrals with their real surface steps.
Why
You see what you'll get before you pick it.
