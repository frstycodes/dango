Icons

Reicon replaces Phosphor everywhere. Its outlines are soft and round-jointed, which suits the dumplings better than Phosphor's straight cuts. Chrome uses Reicon's filled weight, like Discord. Glyphs that are only lines (#, +, arrows) stay lines, because Reicon's filled form boxes them in a tile. The outline weight ships at 1.5 on a 24 grid and is thickened here to 2, matching Gabarito's stem at 16px. Each card shows the glyph at 16px and 40px on light and dark, the Phosphor glyph it replaces, and every place that uses it today.

Channel types

Discord's channel kinds, each with its private (locked) variant and, where Discord has one, its age-restricted variant. The badge is cut from the base glyph by a gap the width of a stroke, so it reads at 16px.

Animated toggles

Mute, deafen and camera are one glyph whose slash draws on and off with the gap beside it, in 200ms. Screen share lifts its arrow on a spring and tints the screen. With reduced motion, the slash appears without drawing. Press a button to toggle it.

Swapping glyphs

When a glyph turns into a different one, the two cross through scale 0.25, a 4px blur and a fade, in about 200ms. With reduced motion only the fade remains.

Other custom glyphs

Concepts Reicon doesn't draw, made on its 24 grid at stroke 2 with continuous squircle corners. Dots are filled so they read as dots.

Gaps and collisions

What Reicon doesn't cover, and the places where several current icons now share one glyph. A shared glyph is fine when the meaning really is the same. Otherwise it needs a call before the swap.
